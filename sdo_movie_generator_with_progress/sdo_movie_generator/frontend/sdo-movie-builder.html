☀️ SDO Daily Movie Generator

Choose an observation period and AIA wavelength. The generator checks NASA's daily movie archive, downloads the available clips, combines them chronologically, and prepares one MP4 file.

The verified legacy archive format is 1024 × 1024 OGV. Other resolutions are not offered until their archive availability is confirmed.
Source: NASA/SDO daily movie archive. Daily movies are pre-generated visual products, not calibrated FITS data. The archive begins on 2010-05-20. Some dates may be missing.
